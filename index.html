How it works

A match is just the beginning

The app makes the introduction. Real life does the rest.

  1. 1

    Get a ticket

    Pick a walk and buy your ticket on Eventfrog. Sign in to meet&vibe with the same email, then add a photo, six interests and three short answers.

  2. 2

    See who's coming

    Until the walk starts, browse the other ticket holders and like the people you'd enjoy meeting. Nobody is ever told who liked them.

  3. 3

    Walk in fours

    The organizer checks you in at the meeting point. Then come five rounds of twenty minutes. Your phone shows your group's number, its colour and a question to get you talking. No perfect opening line needed, just a little curiosity.

  4. 4

    See if you vibe

    Mark each person Friend, Date or Not this time. At ten the next morning you see every mutual choice, with the contact details that person chose to share.

Less small talk. More shared stories.

Four is the friendly number

A one-on-one with a stranger is a lot of pressure. In a group of four nobody has to carry the conversation, and every round brings three new people.

  • Nobody is stuck

    You never spend a round alone with a stranger. When one conversation pauses, another one starts.

  • Up to fifteen new people

    Five rounds with three new people each. That is three times as many people as you would meet in pairs.

  • Built on what you share

    Groups form around shared interests. Likes nudge who walks with whom, but they never decide it.

Your people are out there

One seat each, every round

On a dating app, a handful of profiles collect most of the matches. At a walk, everyone holds one seat in every round, however many likes they got.

Likes help decide who walks with whom, never whether you walk. So the matches spread across the whole group.

Safety

Safe by design

The walk itself is the safety plan: a public route, an organizer on site, groups of four, and no contact details until two people both say yes.

  • Faces checked at the start

    The organizer compares every face with its profile photo before the first round. A fake profile ends at the meeting point.

  • Contact only when it's mutual

    No chat and no phone numbers on profiles. You see someone's contact details only when you both chose to meet again, and only the details they picked.

  • Block once, never grouped again

    Anyone you block or report is never put in your group again, at any walk. If they hold a ticket for your walk, the app tells you first.

  • Women-only groups

    At friends and mixed walks, women can choose to walk only with women.

  • Private by default

    Your profile is visible only to people with a ticket for the same walk. Notifications never show names.

  • Help is one tap away

    During the walk the app shows the emergency number 112 and the organizer's phone number.

Everyone accepts our code of conduct at sign-up. Serious breaches end a membership at once.

FAQ

Good questions

Is meet&vibe a dating app?

It's a way to meet real people in real life. You'll make cool friends, or maybe find your special someone. Friendship is the default. Dating is something you switch on, behind its own consent screen. One switch turns it off again and deletes your dating data. Each walk says on its page whether it is a friends walk, a mixed walk or a dating walk.

Do I need to bring someone?

No. Coming on your own is the idea, and the groups make it easy: a question each round gets the conversation going, so nobody has to break the ice alone. Friends are welcome too. Each of you gets your own ticket and profile. The rounds mix everyone, so you all meet new people, and you can catch up over drinks at the end.

What if nobody likes my profile?

You still walk in a group in every round. Likes help decide who walks with whom, never whether you walk. Groups form mainly around shared interests.

Will I meet someone I click with?

A walk spreads the chances far more evenly than an app. In simulated mixed walks of 40 people, 8% left without a mutual choice. On an app with the same people, 18% would get no match at all. At dating walks of 40 the shares are 15% and 51%.

How are the groups put together?

Around shared interests. Your profile holds six interest tags, and each group forms around the tags its members share. A like counts about as much as one shared interest. Every round brings new people for as long as the walk has enough of them.

Can people see who liked them?

No. Likes only help plan the groups. Nobody is ever told who liked them, and skipping someone is silent.

Is there a chat?

No. Offline is a good place to be. When two people both choose to see each other again, each gets the other's contact card, holding whatever that person chose to share: a phone number, an Instagram handle, an email address or a single line of text.

What does "Not this time" do?

Nothing is revealed to anyone, and the two of you are never put in the same group again.

What if I'm running late?

Check in with the organizer when you arrive and you join the next round. The app re-plans the rounds still ahead, so nobody waits.

What should I bring?

A charged phone and comfortable shoes. A walk takes about two hours: fifteen minutes to arrive and check in, five rounds of twenty minutes, then drinks nearby if you feel like it.

Do I need to install an app?

No. A little online, a lot of real life: meet&vibe runs in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen. You sign in with a six-digit code sent to your email, so there is no password to remember.

Who can join, and what does it cost?

Anyone aged 18 or over, and the organizer may check ID at the walk. Tickets are sold per walk on Eventfrog, where you see the price and the cancellation terms before you buy.
