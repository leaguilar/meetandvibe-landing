A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before we
head out.

What exactly is meet&vibe?+

meet&vibe brings people together for in-person experiences, with a little help from an app. You match online, sign up for an activity, and meet a group in real life to see how you connect.

Do I need to bring someone?+

You can come on your own — that’s the idea. You’ll meet a group of people matched through the app, with a shared activity to get the conversation started.

Where will the first experiences happen?+

We’re starting in Berlin with group walks to discover the city together. Specific meeting points, routes, and dates will be shared when experiences become available.

When can I try the app?+

The app and our first Berlin experiences are coming soon. We’ll share launch details and ways to join here on meetandvibe.com. There’s no waitlist or booking available just yet.
